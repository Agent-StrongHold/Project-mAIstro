  the only implementation; nothing persists a Workspace's mood across restart.
  `SelfModelStateStore` is the declared seam (`backend/state.py`) and the
  restart test asserts the truthful behaviour (fresh composition → defaults).
  AC "durable state survives *where required*" is met by explicit semantics, not
  by a durable Turing store — none exists.

  provisions its canonical Workspace by deterministic uuid5 id, and
  `InMemoryWorkspaceStore.create` raises on a duplicate id. With only

  holds; a *durable shared* workspace store would need a get-then-create. No
  such adapter is composed anywhere in Turing, and `TURING_REPLICA_COUNT > 1`
  refuses to start without a shared `SelfModelStateStore`.
